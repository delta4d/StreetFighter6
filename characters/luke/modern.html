NOTATION GLOSSARY
□ Square = Light (L)
✕ Cross = Medium (M)
○ Circle = Heavy (H)
△ Triangle = Special (S)
R2 R2 = Assist (A) — Modern
L1 L1 = Drive Impact (DI)
Numpad directions
2=↓ 4=← 6=→ 8=↑ 1=↙ 3=↘ 7=↖ 9=↗
66 = Dash
Tap Forward twice quickly to dash forward
214 = QCB
Quarter-circle back: ↓↙← (down → down-back → back)
623 = DP
Dragon Punch: →↓↘ (forward → down → down-forward)
236 = Fireball
Quarter-circle forward: ↓↘→ (down → down-fwd → forward)
JH = Jump Heavy
Jump then press ○ in the air
(JUST)
Just-frame: must input on the exact frame. Very tight timing!
(c) Counter Hit
Hit opponent during their attack startup. Routes change on CH.
(nc) No Counter
This version works without needing counter hit
pc = Punish Counter
Land your move on opponent's unsafe attack
DI = Drive Impact
Press L1 — costs 1 Drive bar. Armored attack that wall-splats.
OD = Overdrive
Press two attack buttons together for enhanced special. Costs 2 Drive bars.
236236 = SA1
Fireball motion twice + button for Super Art Level 1 (1 bar)
214214 = SA2
QCB motion twice + button for Super Art Level 2 (2 bars)
CA = Critical Art
Super Art Level 3 — 236236+○ (3 bars). Most powerful super.
*3 = Repeat ×3
Do the preceding input 3 times in a row
